## Notes on methodology

- **Why long-tail?** New domains (Domain Authority near 0) cannot compete for short generic terms. Phrases of 4+ words have 90% less competition and 3x higher conversion rate.
- **Why these specific phrases?** Sourced from Aneta's own bio language ("coach systemowa", "wzorce rodzinne", "wewnętrzna równowaga", "przewodniczka transformacji") — this guarantees the keywords match her authentic voice, not generic SEO templates.
- **Realistic timeline:** Commercial phrases (#1–4) can rank in 1–3 months. Blog phrases (#5–10) typically take 3–9 months to reach page 1 after the article is published.
- **Localization opportunity:** Adding city names (Warszawa, Kraków, Gdańsk, Wrocław) to any phrase creates an even easier-to-rank variant. Worth doing if Aneta has a physical office or prefers local clients.
